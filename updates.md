## Reading this ledger

- **H-PASS**: historical functional/oracle pass, with the earlier campaign limitations; not a modern strict-background guarantee.
- **Verified GUI / assisted GUI**: independent review confirmed the requested semantic result and observed GUI source. Assisted means operator preparation/recovery was required; it does not prove autonomous startup.
- **Verified file**: disposable artifact checked; not private-account or GUI success. **Memory write** proves persistence; **memory answer / limited** matches saved facts without proving causal retrieval in the latest attempt.
- **Incomplete**: required scope/source/result not verified; partial facts or a delivered tool call are insufficient. **Paused**, **cancelled**, **awaiting review**, **evidence failure** and **failed** remain separate outcomes.
- Fix references are **related prevention/recovery** unless the registry explicitly describes a reproduced failing regression. A related fix does not retroactively pass the original task or establish its exact cause.

## Evidence, privacy and acceptance boundaries

Exact prompts, private answers, screenshots, native/browser identities, account details, artifact hashes and original evidence locations remain in restricted operator records outside Git. This public ledger contains reviewed intent paraphrases, stable case IDs, exact-attempt ordinals, source revisions and outcomes. An authorized reviewer can correlate those IDs with the retained private records. No private inbox/calendar/course roster, credentials, cookies, tokens or browser profiles are included.

Real accounts were read-only. No coursework was done or submitted; assignment bodies, solutions, answers, submissions, personal grades and feedback were outside scope. Deadline and published policy metadata are different from coursework content. No real messages, registrations, applications, purchases or calendar events were sent/created. Write checks used disposable files. Authentication, passkeys and permission approvals remained owner actions; no bypass or credential copying is claimed.

The earlier audit explicitly withdrew broad noninterference claims: system website opening and concurrent active-app protection were **not verified**, and the suite does **not** prove current strict-background policy. One foreground delivery and five app-focus warnings were recorded across the historical batches; those original attempts fail background acceptance. Later retries do not erase them. Ten TextEdit checks did compare actual app contents with files and verify documents closed. Those are useful functional checks, not proof of private Canvas/Handshake accuracy.

Native foreground samples were generally one second apart; they cannot exclude subsecond activation. Earlier automation disabled focus emulation only on an extra Playwright session, leaving the original session able to report a visible renderer while the native window was hidden. The private adapter was corrected, and F06 adds shared CI coverage. Keep native delivery/focus evidence and renderer visibility evidence separate; do not reinterpret every prior result as proven hidden-renderer behavior.
