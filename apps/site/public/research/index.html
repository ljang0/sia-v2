Plain-language notice

Sia research release

The signed-in alpha records eligible task-visible activity so researchers can study how computer agents behave across real work. Google Workspace connector turns are excluded.

Consent version: alpha-research-v3-rawAdults 18+ only
The short version. Research mode uploads raw task-visible activity. Eligible task content may contain private information. Turns that invoke Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Slides are excluded from research capture. Local mode does not upload research data. Connected apps are optional and separately consented.

Your choice

Join research

Sign in, review the current raw-data consent, and accept it before a signed-in task starts. Eligible task events upload to the encrypted research archive.

Continue locally

Use Sia without a cloud account. Local-only records never become eligible for later research upload.

What the research records

The complete activity Sia observes can include exact prompts, replies, surfaced reasoning, commands and output, tool arguments and results, approvals, errors, file paths and diffs, browser and computer activity, and captured images visible inside the task surface. A turn that invokes a Google Workspace connector is excluded in full, including its prompt, connector result, and response.

Raw task content can contain private information or secrets. It is not anonymous. Use research mode only with material you agree to share.

What stays outside the capture surface

Sia does not intentionally obtain provider credentials, Chrome cookies, Keychain contents, secure fields, private-window contents, or hidden authentication surfaces. This reduces exposure, but task-visible content can still contain sensitive information.

Who can review it

Authorized research administrators can inspect eligible raw turns. Google Workspace connector turns never enter that archive. Archive access requires administrator membership and multi-factor authentication. Every administrator read writes an immutable metadata-only audit record.

How long it stays

  • Cloud research content expires after 90 days.
  • Immutable research access audit metadata is retained for 365 days and expires after 400 days.
  • Local records roll off after 90 days or earlier when the encrypted store reaches 128 MB or 500 batches.

How it is used

Researchers use the records to evaluate complete agent behavior, debug failures, improve safety and usability, and operate the alpha. Research data is not used for model training under the current policy.

Connected apps

Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, and Slack are optional. Google starts read-only, with editing and sending enabled through a separate consent step. Each provider connection can be disconnected independently.

A turn that invokes a Google Workspace connector is excluded in full from research capture. Slack and other task-visible content may become part of the research record when research mode is enabled.

Your controls

  • Turn capture off where available or sign out to stop new eligible capture.
  • Export local and cloud research records.
  • Request research-only deletion or full Sia account deletion.
  • Disconnect each connected service independently.

Pending eligible uploads are not silently discarded only because capture is turned off or you sign out. Deleting a Sia account does not delete source data held by connected providers.
