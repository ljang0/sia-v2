How it works

Ask naturally. Stay close to the work.

Sia turns a request into visible, reviewable computer activity instead of making you learn another workflow language.

Describe the outcome

Ask for a file, a browser task, a connected-app read, or work that should happen again later.

Choose the right route

Sia uses a purpose-built connection when one is available and computer use when the desktop itself is the task surface.

Review consequential work

Messages, uploads, sharing, and other writes show the exact target and content before they happen, unless you explicitly choose a broader approval policy.

Keep the record

Activity, approvals, errors, and outcomes stay organized by thread so a run can be inspected rather than guessed at.

Connected apps

Your tools, with their own consent.

Choose only the services you want. Each opens its official provider page, and each can be disconnected independently.

One connection button, clear choices.

Start from Sia, select your apps, choose the provider account or workspace, and approve on the provider's page. Slack does not depend on the desktop Slack app or a plugin.

Recurring work

Say when it should run.

Ask Sia to check, search, monitor, or report on a cadence. The schedule stays attached to the thread, with plain-language controls to pause, change, or remove it.

“Check the web every hour and tell me when the result changes.”

Alpha schedules run locally while Sia is open and the Mac is awake. Sia shows that boundary before relying on the schedule.

Research release

Raw by design. Optional by choice.

Signed-in alpha participation records eligible task-visible activity for agent-behavior research. Google Workspace connector turns are excluded. Local mode remains available without a Sia account or cloud upload.

Task surface

Prompts, replies, reasoning shown to you, commands, tool activity, approvals, errors, computer events, and captured images. Turns that invoke Google Workspace connectors are excluded.
